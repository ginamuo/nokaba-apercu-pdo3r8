The story

A town that almost exists

01The town1 / 5

Welcome to Belhavre, a small seaside town of five thousand set on the edge of its bay, somewhere on Canada’s east coast. Here, the day begins at the harbour. The lighthouse keeps watch over the mouth of the bay, and the fishing boats come back to the wharf while the marina’s sailboats wait for the wind.

Then head up to the central square, where the fountain murmurs at the foot of the town hall’s bell tower. From there, the main street runs on a diagonal past its terraces and shop signs, all the way to the firms and clinics of the professional district. To the west, a green haven welcomes life’s great moments, while to the north, warehouses and concrete blocks set the rhythm of the business park.

Thirteen addresses await you across these six districts, and each one opens the door to a complete website. Just click a marker to begin the tour.

02The making2 / 5

You’ll look for Belhavre on a map in vain. I drew its bay and its streets, imagined its shops and its people, then designed a complete site for every one of its players, treated as if it were due to a real client tomorrow. Structure, content, images, tone and navigation: nothing was left to chance.

Each building in Belhavre stands for a family of trades that carries over to real sectors. The law firm speaks just as well to notaries, accountants and consultants. The physiotherapy clinic can serve as a model for specialized medical practices or wellness centres. The marina meets the digital needs of yacht clubs as well as campgrounds and resorts. The fishers’ cooperative and the funeral cooperative speak to every collective organization, and the tattoo studio to every artist. Is your sector missing from the thirteen sites of the Belhavre universe? The site closest to your line of work will show you, by example, what I can deliver for you.

03The tools3 / 5

A beautiful site draws people in; a useful one brings them back. At most Belhavre addresses, a "Demo tools" page takes you to the other side of the shop window, where you discover what your site could do for you every day while you get on with your work.

The first kind are already built into the sites and ready to use from launch day: calculators, configurators, interactive calendars, questionnaires, forms that guide your customers step by step, and many more. I design and deliver them with your site, with no heavy infrastructure and no extra subscription.

The second kind go much further: secure portals for members, patients or buyers, ticketing, AI assistants, real-time tracking and booking, private galleries, smart estimates, comparison tools, text-message reminders and notices, e-signatures, encrypted document drop-offs, connected appointments, waiting lists, shared calendars, digital membership cards, multi-site schedules, direct invoicing and more.

04Custom-built4 / 5

These tools run on dedicated infrastructure custom-built by OyengaMédia, my partner specialized in software engineering, from your data and the way you work. Go ahead and imagine everything you’d love your new site to do for you!

Try these tools on the demo page as if your business already had them. From the very start, you can picture where your site is headed. Begin with a cutting-edge new site equipped with the features you need most, then add new tools as you grow, without ever starting from scratch.

05The why5 / 5

I built Belhavre on one conviction: the businesses and organizations of our region deserve sites of flawless quality, modern and alive, rooted in local reality and designed for the people who live it. The Belhavre universe exists so you can judge how I put that conviction into practice, without having to take me at my word.

A site is judged by its look, the coherence of the whole, the precision of its message, the ease of its navigation and its technical performance. Explore the Belhavre showcase site that matches or comes closest to your line of work, discover what the architectural art of digital presence means, and you’ll soon know what I can do for you. Your essential trades and services, your rare know-how and your singular stories deserve a digital presence worthy of what they are and what they offer. I can design it for you. All you need now is an access code.
