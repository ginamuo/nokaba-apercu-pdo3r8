Service No. 2

Consulting

Communications work when someone has thought them through first.

The intent

Don’t own your communications, and someone else will own your story.

Messages go missing. Materials pull in every direction, and the image turns hazy. Teams wear themselves out producing work with no sense of why, and the audience drifts toward other voices.

Think it through, and everything shifts. Messages do their job, each piece backs up the next, and the organization moves faster because it knows where it’s headed.

I consult on set mandates, with defined deliverables and a schedule I hold to, so you can structure, rebuild or steer your communications and keep them coherent for the long run.

The mandates

Three kinds of mandate

Each one comes with its own scope, deliverables and timeline, all settled before the work starts.

  1. 01

    Audit and recommendations

    Ready to take stock of where your communications stand, and figure out where to steer them next?

    I run a full diagnostic across your materials, your digital presence, your messaging and where you sit with the people you’re trying to reach. You come away with a written report: findings, priorities, concrete recommendations, and an action plan you can put to work right away.

    It fits organizations that grew fast and never stopped to structure their communications, or that sense a change of course coming without knowing where to begin.

    Typical length
    Four to six weeks
    Deliverables
    A complete audit file: the detailed report and a working action plan. The specifics live in your tailored quote.
  2. 02

    Overhaul and alignment

    Rethinking your communications from the ground up, pulling several entities under one banner, reworking your identity, or bringing your message in line with a new strategic direction?

    I run this project alongside you, from setting the new positioning to producing the materials that matter most, and I stay on it until they’re out in the world. We agree on the exact scope up front. A new website, or a rebuilt one, is a mandate of its own.

    It fits organizations in transition: merging, changing name, opening up to new audiences, or standing behind a new vision that has to carry across everything they produce.

    Typical length
    Three to six months
    Deliverables
    A complete overhaul file: strategic positioning, brand guidelines, the priority materials rebuilt, and a rollout schedule. The specifics live in your tailored quote.
  3. 03

    Ongoing support

    A full-time communications hire is more than you need, but a steady outside read on your decisions and your output would help?

    I stay with you over time, on a monthly retainer framed in advance and sized to your real workload and your priorities.

    It fits organizations that want their communications in order without a new hire, or that want outside expertise backing a team already in place.

    Typical length
    A six-month to one-year commitment, renewable.
    Deliverables
    A monthly progress file, shaped around the retainer you choose. The specifics live in your tailored quote.

The practice

How I work

My consulting runs on the same spirit as my web design work. One person across the table, close attention, a careful finish on every deliverable, and full transparency about what’s agreed, what’s underway and what’s still ahead.

I come in to structure, strengthen or steer your communications, whichever the mandate calls for. I don’t handle live crisis communications, online reputation management, or daily social media content. That work belongs to dedicated specialists.

Editorial structure, brand coherence, a medium-term communications strategy, a team rallied around one clear, confident message: that is where I come in, and where I do my best work.

The trades

The sectors I serve

I’m based in New Brunswick, and I work in French and in English, with no rushed translation from one version to the other.

My consulting mandates are built mainly for nonprofit organizations, economic development bodies, professional associations, cooperatives, firms and independent professionals, along with family businesses and small and mid-sized businesses that want their communications in order without staffing a full role in-house.
