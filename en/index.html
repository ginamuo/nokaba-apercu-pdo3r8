The studio

An atelier built for bespoke work

Nokaba is a studio at human scale, run by one person. Every project that leaves it, a website or a consulting mandate alike, is designed and built by the same hands, held to a single standard from the first detail to the last.

That way of working changes a lot for you. No salesperson makes promises here, and no subcontractor falls short of them. You work with one person, start to finish. I study your trade, carry the project through, and stay your point of contact well past launch.

I deliver sites that look like you: striking, technically current, built to win people over. I also deliver consulting mandates that give your communications a real backbone and the clarity they have been missing.

Step into Belhavre

Belhavre appears on no map. This small Acadian town exists nowhere in New Brunswick. I invented it whole, because a carousel of screenshots will never match the feel of clicking through a real site. Every building opens onto a site I designed and built to the last detail, as if the business behind it truly existed.

The town runs as an ecosystem. Each district has its own mood, each address its own story. Modest projects stand next to bolder ones, and new places will open as the studio grows. You get a living demonstration of what I can deliver, and a real way to picture your own project before it exists.

Belhavre is not open to the public. The sites inside it took weeks to build, and I keep access for people seriously considering working together. Ask, and I will open a private three-day pass to the building that matches your line of work.

The Belhavre universe keeps growing. New entities join it as I go.

My approach

Six steps, from the first conversation to the long life of your project

Website or consulting mandate, the method is the same. Here are the main steps.

  1. 01

    Discovery

    You see exactly what I can do, by walking through the Belhavre universe.

  2. 02

    Listening

    We talk at length about your trade, your ambitions, and your constraints. Everything that follows rests on that first conversation.

  3. 03

    Design

    I bring you a full direction, whether visual, editorial, or strategic, and you approve it before I build a thing.

  4. 04

    Build

    I build your project week by week, sharing interim versions you shape as we go.

  5. 05

    Delivery

    I put in place what we agreed and hand you the keys. From there you run it yourself, or you leave the upkeep to me under a dedicated retainer.

  6. 06

    Support

    I stay your point of contact for tweaks and future changes. Depending on what you need, that happens à la carte, or under the maintenance retainer you chose at delivery.
