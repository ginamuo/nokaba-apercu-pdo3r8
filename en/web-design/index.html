Service No. 1

Websites worthy of your ambitions

I build digital work that does your practice justice.

The point

A first impression only happens once.

These days it lands in under ten seconds, on the screen of a phone.

A dated, slow, or ill-built site tells the wrong story before you say a word. Neglect breeds doubt. Absence hands the room to your rivals. A site made with care argues your case around the clock, even while you sleep, and draws the right clients in before they ever meet you.

I build that kind of site. Each one is one of a kind, shaped around your trade and your ambitions.

The delivery

What I deliver

A complete website, from positioning to launch. Here is what that includes.

  1. 01

    A bespoke visual direction

    Palette, type, icons, a graphic language that holds together, all chosen to reflect who you are and reach the people you serve. Nothing off the shelf.

  2. 02

    A clear structure

    A map built for your visitors. A clear page hierarchy, a path that makes sense, calls to action set right where they belong. Every page earns its place and asks for a next step.

  3. 03

    Writing that carries its weight

    I work every line until it says what counts, in a voice that sounds like you. Clarity, precision, and rhythm all get the attention they deserve.

  4. 04

    A modern build

    Clean code, fast pages, mobile handled top to bottom, current standards for accessibility and search. Search engines judge your site too, not only the people reading it.

  5. 05

    A site you run yourself

    Change your text, publish articles, swap out images, all without waiting on me or facing a new invoice. Rather hand those updates off? A maintenance plan covers them.

  6. 06

    A full launch

    Hosting set up in your name, your domain wired in, professional email configured if you want it, security certificates in place. You own every piece. Nothing locked away.

  7. 07

    Hands-on training

    One session, by video call or in person, your call. Video sessions are recorded so you can revisit them, and you receive a written guide made for your own project. You leave able to run the whole thing on your own.

Straight talk

What I don’t build

Let me be upfront about what sits outside my offer. I don’t build complex technical platforms, heavy business portals with advanced databases, multi-user applications, or bulky internal systems. That work calls for deep engineering.

If your project needs that kind of infrastructure, I can point you to my partner OyengaMédia, specialists in software engineering, who can build it. I’ll then coordinate with them on integrating it into the site I design for you.

My expertise lies in designing sites and portals equipped with light, fully functional tools, building a strong visual identity, and structuring clear, optimized communication.

In both languages

Bilingual from the start

Every site can ship in French and English from day one, each language held to the same standard. In New Brunswick, that dual presence is no afterthought. The francophone community here is large and active, and tired of being handed English-only sites or careless translations. A truly bilingual site, where each language gets equal care, tells your Acadian clients that you respect them and that you count them in, even when they’re bilingual themselves.
